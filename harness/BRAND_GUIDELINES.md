# GhostCode Brand Guidelines

## Name

- Product name: GhostCode, written as one word with a capital G and C.
- CLI: `ghostcode`, short alias `ghost`.
- Home directory: `~/.ghostcode`, override with `GHOSTCODE_HOME`.

## Relationship to DeepSeek Harness

GhostCode is a fork of DeepSeek Harness, which is released under the MIT
License. "DeepSeek Harness" is a registered trademark of DeepSeek, so follow
these rules:

- Describe the relationship truthfully, for example "a fork of DeepSeek Harness"
  or "built on DeepSeek Harness".
- Do not use "DeepSeek" or "DeepSeek Harness" in the GhostCode name, logo, or
  sub-project names.
- Do not use DeepSeek logos or brand materials in a way that suggests DeepSeek
  endorses, partners with, or authorized GhostCode.
- Keep the DeepSeek copyright line in `LICENSE` and keep
  `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.

## Plugins

Tag plugin repositories with the `ghostcode-plugin` GitHub topic so others can
find them.
